## Behavior Rules

- Do ONLY what is explicitly asked. Nothing more.
- Do NOT add extra styles, classes, comments, refactors, or improvements unless requested.
- Do NOT reorganize, rename, or reformat code that wasn't mentioned.
- If you think something additional is necessary or beneficial, STOP and ask before doing it.
- Before making any change outside the direct scope of the request, state: "I want to also do X because Y — should I?"
- Never assume silence = permission to expand scope.
